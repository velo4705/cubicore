Walkthrough

From "build a thing" to a working product.

$ /cubicore:plan-full “habit tracker — localStorage, streaks, week view”

› What does success look like?
› Disjoint writes — verified
› Factory-fit form sent to founder
› Waiting for founder to pick · small-factory · 2 associates
$ /cubicore:execute
› associates streaming in your opencode session
› checks: schema ✓ verify ✓ scope ✓
$ /cubicore:debug
› 1 check failed — fixed, re-ran, green
1

Plan

Cubicore reads your repo, proposes a design direction (or asks you), and writes CUBICORE_PLAN.md. Nothing runs yet.

2

Factory-fit

You receive one question in a form: team of N, or single agent? Your answer decides the split and the budget.

3

Execute

Associates spawn under your session (as opencode sub-agents), write disjoint files, and report results natively.

4

Gates

Every contract must pass its own checks on disk before it's marked done. No signal without evidence.

5

Integrate & preview

Cubicore runs the integration wave, serves the result, and reports what you can click on.

6

Retrospect

Tokens, verdict, what stayed vs. what was discarded. The ledger is the proof.
