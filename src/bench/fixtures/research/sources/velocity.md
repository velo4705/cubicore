# On delivery speed

Teams that ship weekly accumulate small corrections. Teams that ship quarterly
accumulate large ones. The compounding is not in the code -- it is in the
feedback loop. A one-week loop means the cost of a wrong decision is one week
of work. A one-quarter loop means it is a quarter.

This is not a claim that speed is free. It is a claim that speed changes the
price of being wrong, and the price is what determines how carefully anyone
thinks before deciding.
